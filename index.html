Coach speak & the clock

NBA injury reports drop three times on game days: 1:30, 5:30, and 8:30 PM Eastern. If your lineup locks before the last one, you're betting blind. Tonight is the proof: Houston hadn't even filed a report at 8 this morning because of the back-to-back, and Sengun went from watch-list to cleared by the 1:30 drop. Jaylen Brown's final call in Charlotte lands at the 5:30 report. NFL reports land Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday, and the final game-day designations are the only ones that count.

The quote that matters today is Nurse on Brown: the timetable is treatment and pain tolerance, which in fantasy language means check the 5:30 drop, not the calendar. With at least three teams on the second night of a back-to-back, the rest-day dominoes are the real lineup news on this seven-game Halloween slate.

Sources: PhillyVoice (Adam Aaronson) and RotoBaller, Oct 31, 2026
