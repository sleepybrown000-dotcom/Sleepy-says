Keenan Allen is the week’s top waiver wideout, but his situation carries inherent risk: flex consideration in a six-bye week, not a must-start. Darren Waller and Jauan Jennings round out the popular decisions. Mike Evans is a game-time call in Detroit with a real shot to return from the Achilles.

Tight ends

There are very few instances where you bench Travis Kelce in a six-bye week. Dallas Goedert is a reasonable TE1/2 bet coming off the Eagles’ bye. Isaiah Likely is the Baltimore answer ESPN projects to finish as TE1.
