The hardwood: injury watch

Two weeks into the NBA season and the injury bug bit hard. Here's who's down, who to grab, and what the coaches are saying.

Out ~2 months

Anthony Edwards, Timberwolves

Arthroscopic knee surgery after a 47-point OT gem in Cleveland. Stash him on IR if you have the spot; he's aiming for a second-half return.

The adds: Ayo Dosunmu (75% rostered) is the next man up; if he's gone, Terrence Shannon Jr. (20%) is averaging 13 points and 2.5 threes in 22 minutes.

Source: ESPN (Shams Charania) via Yahoo Fantasy, Oct 30, 2026

Out, no timetable

Tyrese Haliburton, Pacers

A left hamstring strain on top of the Achilles comeback, after a 21.8 / 9.5 start. The Pacers will be cautious. Nembhard takes the keys; Pascal Siakam's workload grows.

The adds: Andrew Nembhard everywhere. Deep leagues: Quenton Jackson (1% rostered) if this drags on.

Source: ESPN (Shams Charania) via Yahoo Fantasy, Oct 30, 2026

Out 4+ weeks

Ja Morant, Trail Blazers

Grade 2 hamstring strain, right as Portland was cooking. His usage flows to Lillard and Avdija, with Holiday and Henderson splitting the backcourt minutes.

The adds: Jrue Holiday (48% rostered, the steady option) or Scoot Henderson (9%) if you need upside in deep leagues.

Source: Yahoo Fantasy (Dan Titus), Oct 30, 2026

Out 4–6 weeks

Paolo Banchero, Magic

Low-grade high-ankle sprain, and Jalen Suggs is out at least two more weeks with a knee bone bruise. Franz Wagner eats first; the waiver gets interesting behind him.

The adds: Anthony Black (68%) came back with 18 points, 5 threes, 5 assists. Tristan da Silva (22%) is playing 28 minutes a night.

Source: Yahoo Fantasy (Dan Titus), Oct 30, 2026

Re-evaluated next week

Coach speak & the clock

NBA injury reports drop three times on game days: 1:30, 5:30, and 8:30 PM Eastern. If your lineup locks before the last one, you're betting blind. NFL reports land Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday, and the final game-day designations are the only ones that count.

This week's quote that matters: Steichen on Downs, above. Full-practice Fridays are how contenders tell you who's playing without telling you.
